### What's New in Project X v1.3.0 🚀

- 🎨 **Dynamic Material You Theme**: Fixed wallpaper-adaptive color extraction for Infinix, Tecno, and all Android 12+ devices.
- ⏱️ **Refined Focus Presets**: Simplified standard presets to **15m**, **20m**, **45m**, and **Custom Duration** with interactive minute slider.
- 💬 **Dynamic Motivational Quotes**: Over 40+ focus prompts that appear **only when the timer is running** and rotate randomly every 30 seconds.
- 💡 **Long Session Fatigue Reminder**: Helpful pacing guidance when choosing 45m+ focus blocks to optimize knowledge absorption.
- 🎯 **Configurable Daily Study Goal**: Set and adjust your daily progress limit in Settings (defaults to 45 min). Daily streak increments upon reaching your target!
- ⚡ **Instant Updater Feedback**: Immediate "Gathering info" visual status upon clicking update, with real-time download progress and direct install screen.
- 🛡️ **In-App Crash Diagnostic Screen**: Retained isolated crash reporting to protect against unhandled issues.
